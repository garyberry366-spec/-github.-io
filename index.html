CUSTOM ORDER TERMS

Order & Payment Terms

Simple, clear expectations for every custom build.

  • All custom builds are made to order.
  • A final quote will be provided before work begins.
  • Build time is typically 3–4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the project.
  • Payment arrangements will be confirmed before the build begins.
  • Custom work, special parts, electronics, trailers, and highly detailed requests may change the final price.
  • Changes requested after the build has started may result in additional charges.
  • Shipping costs, if applicable, will be included in the final quote.
  • By submitting a custom order request, you acknowledge that project details and pricing will be confirmed with Ryan Smith Custom Builder before construction begins.
